# Silver Price Today Date: 27th Dec 2025
Silver in India has always had a life beyond investment charts. Walk into any household and you’ll find it - coins bought on Dhanteras, small bowls used in rituals, or jewellery gifted at weddings. It’s part of tradition, which is why people still look up the silver rate today almost as routinely as checking the weather.The silver price in India isn’t steady. It moves with international markets, changes in industrial demand, and the rupee’s exchange rate against the dollar. What makes silver different from gold is this dual pull. On one side, it’s a festival metal, rooted in culture. On the other, it’s an industrial commodity, used in solar panels, mobile phones, even electric vehicles. That mix keeps the silver price moving in ways gold doesn’t.Rates also shift by location. The silver price in Delhi may not exactly match what you’d find in Chennai or Mumbai. Local taxes, transport costs, and jewellers’ margins explain the difference. Small if you’re buying a pair of anklets, noticeable if you’re investing in larger bars or coins.And that’s why following silver prices matters. For some, it’s about tradition. For others, it’s about timing an investment. For most, it’s both.

The silver price today isn’t something that moves on guesswork. It reacts to real forces, both global and local. When industries across the world buy more silver for solar panels, electronics, or even electric vehicles, prices here in India usually climb. And since most of our silver is imported, a strong dollar or a weak rupee can quickly make every gram costlier. On the flip side, when international markets slow down or investors lose interest in commodities, silver rates soften.

In India, there’s another layer — tradition. Around Diwali, during weddings, or even on Akshaya Tritiya, the demand for coins and jewellery shoots up. Walk into a jeweller’s shop in those weeks, and you’ll notice how silver prices today often edge higher compared with quieter months.
